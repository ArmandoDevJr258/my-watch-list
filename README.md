## MY watch List

this is an open source mobile app for Finding , tracking, and in the future realeses watch tV shows ..
feel free to DM me on Insta or LinkedIn for Colab..



## Features

Users can:

* **Search Shows:** Easily find TV shows using the search input.
* **Watch Trailers:** Watch trailers of any selected show directly from the app.
* **Add to Favorites & Watchlist:** Save shows to your Favorites or Currently Watching list.
* **View Show Details:** Access detailed information, including cast, title, summary, rating, and genres.
* **Manage Watchlist:** In the **My Watchlist** tab, users can view show details, watch trailers, and track their watching progress.
* **Customize App Appearance:** Adjust app themes and fonts in the Settings tab.
* **Get Help & Support:** Access guidance and support whenever needed.
